# 🚀 Kuchh Bhii v2.1.0 — World-Class UI/UX & Ambient AI Edition

This release delivers a comprehensive product and design transformation: collapsing 11 fragmented tabs into an intuitive **4-Pillar Mobile Cockpit**, reimagining the AI from a text-heavy chatbot into an **Ambient Intelligence Copilot**, and introducing high-retention behavioral mechanics including the **0630 Morning Stand-Up**, **Tactile Web Audio & Haptics**, and **Ghost Runner Pacing**.

---

### 🌟 Key Changes in v2.1.0

#### 1. 🧭 4-Pillar Bottom Navigation with Central `+` Action FAB
* **Consolidated Architecture**:
  * **Today Cockpit** (`/playbook`): Daily status, ambient copilot, athletic battery, and today's session.
  * **Train & Live Tracking** (`/activities`): Direct access to live GPS tracking, football cockpit, pitch armor, and maps.
  * **Progress & Telemetry** (`/fithub`): Multi-year Google Fit analytics (1,456 days), authentic Strava timeline, and milestones.
  * **Vault & Habits** (`/discipline`): Daily discipline checklist, fuel & nutrition, Vedas, cycle sync, and settings.
* **Central Floating Action Button (`+`)**: A raised, high-visibility neon-gradient button on mobile and header for one-tap activity logging (Live GPS Run/Cycle, Football Match, Push-ups/Calisthenics, Daily Recap Card).
* **Sub-Pillar Quick Switchers**: Clean horizontal pill bars at the top of Train, Progress, and Vault views for zero-friction sub-navigation.

#### 2. ⚡ Ambient Intelligence Copilot Pill
* **"Show, Don't Tell; Act, Don't Chat"**: Replaced the 400px text-heavy accordion with an ultra-sleek 56px ambient copilot pill.
* **Live Telemetry & Directive**: Real-time readiness score (`95% Optimal`), active streak, and dynamic 1-sentence athletic directives.
* **1-Tap Headphone Audio Brief**: Directly triggers a 10-second spoken coach directive via `speakText` before you step onto the track or pitch.
* **Dedicated Slide-Over Copilot Sheet**: Interactive dialogue, tactical advice, and custom prompt chips open in a distraction-free modal without pushing down the screen.

#### 3. 🌅 0630 Morning Stand-Up & Daily Trinity Rings
* **Athletic Battery Gauge**: Real-time recovery score calculated from recent strain and streak consistency.
* **The 3 Target Rings**:
  * **Movement Ring**: Progress toward daily km and aerobic volume.
  * **Discipline Ring**: Live percentage of habits completed today.
  * **Hydration Ring**: Visual water tracker with quick `+500ml` logging.
* **1-Tap Muster Call (+25 XP)**: Confirms morning roll call and plays a ceremonial military bugle fanfare.

#### 4. 🔊 Tactile Sound Effects & Haptics Engine
* **Mechanical Switch Click-Thud**: Synthesized tactile audio feedback when checking off habits in the daily checklist.
* **Level-Up Fanfare**: Multi-chord victory chime when completing sessions or finishing all daily habits.
* **Referee Whistle**: Dual-tone match whistle for football session logging.
* **Built-in Soundscapes**:
  * **`175 SPM Cadence Metronome`**: Audio runner metronome for cadence lock.
  * **`432Hz Vedic Focus`**: Continuous meditative drone for stretching and mobility.
* **Haptics**: Native vibration pulses delivered to supported mobile devices.

#### 5. 👻 Ghost Runner Real-Time Pacing
* Real-time benchmark comparison during live GPS tracking against your 4:40/km course PR (*"⚡ Ahead of Target Pace by +42m!"*).
* Finish celebration with multi-tone victory fanfare and immediate launch of the 3D satellite flyby reel.

#### 6. 🎨 Visual De-Textualization
* **Football Pitch Board**: 2D tactical pitch canvas with cone setups, animated runner paths, and a 1-tap `[ Listen to Coach ]` audio button.
* **Segmented Habits**: Daily habits cleanly separated from military cadre and the excuse buster terminal.
* **AI Social Captions**: 1-tap caption chips (Anfield banter, military ethos, minimalist stats) directly inside the activity detail view.
